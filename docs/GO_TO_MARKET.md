# Go-to-market — selling Orbyt Verify on a small scale

This is the wedge: small, sellable now, and a path up to the bigger regulated platform later.

## Who buys it (and feels the pain today)

1. **Staffing / recruiting agencies placing remote contractors** — they get burned by fake candidates and it's their reputation on the line. Fast, repeat buyers.
2. **Security/IT teams at companies hiring remote engineers** — the "did we just hire a North Korean operative?" fear is real and current (Amazon stopped 1,800+ suspected DPRK operatives; KnowBe4 famously hired one). CISOs have budget for fraud prevention.
3. **Technical recruiting teams / RPOs** running high-volume remote interviews.
4. **Marketplaces / gig platforms** onboarding remote workers.

Start with #1 and #2 — shortest path to a check.

## The pitch (one sentence)

"A 60-second link your candidate completes that flags deepfakes, virtual cameras, VPN/relay, and impersonation — so you don't hire a fake remote worker. It's fraud detection, not a background check, so there's no compliance lift to start."

## Pricing (pick one to start)

- **Per-verification:** $5–$15 each (your cost is ~$0 without IDV, ~$1.50 with Stripe Identity). High margin.
- **Seat/subscription:** $99–$499/mo per recruiting team for up to N checks, then per-overage.
- **Land-and-expand:** free pilot (50 checks) → paid. The dashboard already counts "this month · billable".

Charge from day one — even $99/mo from a design partner validates more than 100 "interested" replies.

## First 10 customers (do this, in order)

1. **Reframe to the wedge** — you're selling "fake-candidate detection," not background checks.
2. **5–10 buyer conversations first.** Recruiting leads, staffing-agency owners, CISOs. Ask: "How do you know a remote candidate is who they say? Have you been burned?" Listen for their words; put those words on the landing page.
3. **One attorney consult** (~$1–2k) — see `LEGAL_POSITIONING.md`. Do this before you invoice.
4. **Turn on IPQualityScore** (free) so VPN/relay detection is live — that's the demo that sells.
5. **Run a free pilot** with 1–2 friendly buyers on their real candidates. Get a testimonial + a "this caught one" story.
6. **Convert to paid**, then use the case study to land the next 5.

## The story that closes

Real, current, and scary: state-backed and freelance fraud rings place fake remote workers using stolen identities, VPN relays, and deepfaked interviews. Companies are getting hit *now*. You sell the cheap insurance against it — and unlike incumbents, you ship in a day with no compliance battle.

## The build-up to "big daddy"

Orbyt Verify funds and de-risks the bigger play. Once you have revenue + pilots + a security reputation, you expand into the full consent-based screening platform (the regulated CRA product) — *that's* when you take on the legal/data battles, with money and customers behind you. Keep the two products clean and separate: Verify never touches records.
